# The Breach

## Overview

The Breach is an interactive cybersecurity portfolio.

Instead of reading about me, visitors investigate a fictional cyberattack that erased my digital identity.

By solving cybersecurity challenges, they recover memories and slowly reconstruct who I am.

Every solved challenge unlocks another chapter of my journey.
